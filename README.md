# AdminKind Website Review Pack

This folder contains a first draft one-page website for a business offering online IT and life admin support.

## Current Positioning

AdminKind helps older people, families, and busy professionals with online tasks that waste time or feel confusing.

The service currently focuses on:

- Device and app support
- Online forms and accounts
- Broadband, mobile, utilities, and service comparisons
- Life admin that drags on
- Scam sense-checks
- Plain-English decision summaries

## Files

- `index.html` - the website page
- `styles.css` - all styling
- `assets/hero-admin-workspace.png` - hero image used by the site

## How To Review

Open `index.html` in a browser.

Helpful feedback areas:

- Is the target audience clear?
- Does the offer feel trustworthy?
- Does the blend of older people, families, and busy professionals work?
- Is the IT/admin/comparison support proposition easy to understand?
- What should be renamed, removed, or made sharper before launch?
