About the founder

Built from the calls families already recognise.

I am Jay Brook, and AdminKind grew out of a very familiar pattern: calls from my older parents when a phone setting, an online account, a form, or a confusing message needed another pair of eyes.

At the same time, I kept seeing how much of my own week disappeared into life admin: comparing mobile deals, checking broadband options, sorting car insurance quotes, chasing renewals, and trying to make sensible decisions from too many tabs.

That time should have gone to my kids, my work, and the parts of life that actually matter. So AdminKind exists to give people calm, practical help with online tasks, tech questions, provider choices, and the admin that can quietly become another job.
